# TripAtlas Privacy Policy **Last updated: October 2026** TripAtlas is designed with privacy in mind. Your personal reports and experience data are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to TripAtlas or stored on TripAtlas servers. ## Information stored by the app TripAtlas allows you to voluntarily record information such as: - substances - doses - dates and times - location descriptions - social setting - mindset - effects and after-effects - mood - timeline notes - other information you choose to enter in a report This information may be sensitive or health-related. ## How your data is used The information you enter is used only to provide TripAtlas features on your device, including: - displaying your reports - showing your personal statistics and history - allowing you to edit and review previous reports - creating backups when you choose to export your data TripAtlas does not use your personal report data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or marketing. ## Data collection and sharing TripAtlas does not require an account. TripAtlas does not collect or transmit your personal report data to the developer. TripAtlas does not sell your personal data. TripAtlas does not share your personal report data with third parties. If you choose to use the backup or sharing features, you control where the exported backup file is sent or stored. Once you share or save a backup outside TripAtlas, that file is subject to the privacy and security practices of the service or location you choose. ## Local storage Your reports are stored in the application's private local storage on your device. Temporary backup files may be created when you use the export or import functionality. TripAtlas limits and removes temporary backup files as part of its backup handling. Removing the app may result in the loss of locally stored data unless you have created your own backup. ## Permissions TripAtlas does not require access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or other sensitive device permissions for its core functionality. ## Substance and health information TripAtlas provides educational and harm-reduction information. TripAtlas is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Information shown in the app, including substance, dose, interaction, duration, and harm-reduction information, is provided for general informational purposes only and should not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional. ## Data deletion You can delete individual reports within TripAtlas. You can also use the **Delete all app data** option in Settings to remove personal report data stored by TripAtlas on your device. You can additionally remove locally stored TripAtlas data by uninstalling the application. ## Children TripAtlas is not intended for children. ## Changes to this Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated if TripAtlas features or data practices change. The latest version will be made available through the app and the official TripAtlas privacy policy page. ## Contact For privacy-related questions about TripAtlas, contact the developer using the contact information provided on the TripAtlas Google Play Store listing.
